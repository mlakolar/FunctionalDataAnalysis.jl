Roadmap

  • Milestone 0: package skeleton, CI, docs.
  • Milestone 1 (done): data types (grid, irregular, basis, multivariate), conversions, Tables.jl input, simulation generators.
  • Milestone 2 (in progress): per-curve local polynomials and penalized splines are implemented. Smoothing returns grid or basis data with a copied metadata named tuple retaining tuning settings. Common-lambda GCV stores the candidate grid, summed per-curve scores, and selected lambda in metadata.selection. StatsAPI.fit entry points return the same functional data objects. Pooled means use StatsAPI.fit(SmoothedMean, ...), with observation or subject weighting. CurveCV selects bandwidths by leaving out whole curves and stores candidates, errors, and fold assignments on the result. Fixed-bandwidth mean tests match fdapace for both weighting schemes. Covariances use StatsAPI.fit(SmoothedCovariance, fd, mean; ...), with off-diagonal product-kernel local linear smoothing, optional time binning, curve-level CV conditional on the supplied mean, bilinear evaluation, and rotated full-interval noise variance (selected in milestone 5, issue #17). The original surface-diagonal, middle-half rule remains explicit. diagonal_bandwidth defaults to the covariance bandwidth and may be set separately as a number or CurveCV. The mean bandwidth and diagonal_bandwidth also accept KernelSmoothing regression BandwidthSelectors and pilot-centred CurveCV(; pilot, multipliers); the 2D covariance bandwidth needs a number or explicit CurveCV candidates. Both weighting schemes match fixed-bandwidth fdapace covariance fixtures. Mean uncertainty is available through the milestone 5 curve bootstrap and KernelSmoothing curve-cluster sandwich intervals.
  • Milestone 3 (done): dense univariate FPCA is implemented, with a stored weighted eigenbasis, quadrature scores, FVE or fixed component selection, optional per-curve presmoothing, prediction, and reconstruction. The dense default component cap is max(1, min(length(grid)-2, nobs(data)-2)), following the fdapace source audit and replacing the plan's cap of 20. Source-audited fdapace fixtures test the documented eigenvalue convention. PACE now reuses pooled mean and covariance smoothing, accepts supplied means, surfaces, and noise variance, and stores conditional-expectation scores and their conditional covariances. FVE, fixed K, AIC, and BIC are supported; AIC/BIC retain the full criterion curve and global minimizer alongside fdapace's first-increase choice. Sparse prediction reuses the fitted mean, eigenfunctions, full projected covariance, and noise variance. No-keyword fits use source-audited PACE defaults: a 51-point work grid, Gaussian kernel, observation weighting, and mean/covariance bandwidths of 5%/10% of the observed range. Every choice is retained and shown. pace_bandwidth_candidates exposes fdapace's geometric candidates for optional whole-curve CV. Automatic binning follows the audited bin-count rule and reuses the existing weighted covariance-pair aggregation. End-to-end fixed-bandwidth and default references, sparse recovery, dense-versus-PACE agreement, and a bounded-time sparse fit complete the milestone validation. Intentional fdapace differences are documented on the FPCA and audit pages. Roughness-penalized FPCA, FACE, and conditional score rho regularization remain outside this milestone's current scope.
  • Milestone 4 (done in FDA): marginal multivariate FPCA delegates to dense or PACE fits per node. Pooled complete-grid FPCA uses the average node sample covariance, a shared weighted eigenbasis, and node-specific means and score variances. score_array provides subject × node × component order for FunctionalGraphicalModels.jl. CrossCovariance returns exact dense sample cross-covariances; SmoothedCrossCovariance reuses the local linear smoother with scalar or separate axis bandwidths and subject CV. cross_covariances returns diagonal covariance and transposed cross blocks, optionally using task 1 FPCA means. Same-time pairs are kept under independent cross-node noise, or explicitly excluded. Happ–Greven MFPCA combines the marginal scores through a weighted joint eigenproblem, with prediction, reconstruction, unequal domains/designs, and R MFPCA 1.3-11 validation. FGM estimator migration is maintained in the FGM repository. See Multivariate FPCA validation for defaults and checks. See Cross-covariance validation for task 3. See Multivariate FPCA (MFPCA) for task 4, its source audit, defaults, and validation.
  • Milestone 5 (in progress): see and trust the fit. Task 1 implements a plotting-library-independent coordinate layer and RecipesBase recipes for Plots.jl, including observed data, means, modes, scree plots, covariance surfaces, observation designs, and fitted paths. See Plotting for defaults, simulated figures, and the fdapace 0.6.0 source audit. Task 2 adds exact derivative bases, polynomial curve/mean derivatives, FPC and DPC derivative fits, reconstruction and nodewise plotting. The derivative reference audit documents the consistent weighted DPC quantity and its departures from fdapace. Task 3 adds conditional PACE fitted-curve intervals and simultaneous bands, curve-bootstrap mean intervals and sup-t bands, curve-cluster sandwich mean intervals, and ribbon recipes. See Uncertainty. Task 4 adds six classic datasets through a lazy artifact, with offline samples and source/licence provenance. DTI retains both cca and rcst on separate grids, with masks and scan/subject covariates. See Datasets. Task 5 adds the rotated noise diagonal (fdapace PC_CovE; Yao, Müller and Wang 2005), full-interval integration and the Discovery benchmark in benchmark/sigma2/ (issue #17); the rotated diagonal over the full interval is now the default. Makie recipes are deferred. This scope follows the Tier 1 Gap Map and Mladen's 24 October decision, replacing the original milestone 5 scope.
  • Milestone 6 (in progress): scale and cross-structure. Dense-grid FACE is available through fit(FPCA, data; method=:face, face=(; ...)), including an internal coefficient-difference P-spline building block, GCV, missing-data iteration, shrinkage scores, pointwise posterior bands, and marginal/MFPCA reuse. The source audit and scale/variance departures from refund 0.1-40 are documented on the FPCA page. The cross-structure task adds weighted FSVD with dense integration and joint PACE scores, concurrent correlation through five local linear smooths, and dynamic correlation; see Cross-structure. Roughness-penalized basis FPCA reuses the FPCA result and eigenstep, with exact basis prediction/reconstruction and explicit leave-one-curve-out lambda selection with an explicit fixed component count. Functional CCA adds paired basis weights, variates, penalized squared correlations, and optional leading-variate CV. The fda 6.3.0 reference and defaults are on the FPCA and CCA pages. Sparse FACE is implemented for irregular data, including spline covariance/noise estimation, conditional scores and prediction; see sparse FACE. Multi-penalty REML belongs to milestone 7.
  • Milestone 7 (in progress): functional regression. FPC scalar-on-function regression supports multiple dense, sparse or basis covariates, optional scalar covariates, supplied marginal FPCA fits, leading-count selection, prediction and conditional pointwise intervals. See Regression. Gaussian multi-penalty REML/GCV and penalized regression are subsequent tasks.
  • Milestone 8: depth, outliers, and clustering.
  • Milestone 9: registration.
